# 00 — Vision

**Status:** draft v1 (2026-09-23)

## One-sentence pitch

> AI assistants recommend whatever they read on the first pages of the internet about your sector.
> We tell a clinic which pages those are, where it is missing, what to publish, and how many patients arrive because of it.

## Problem

Patients increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Google's AI answers "which is the best dental clinic in Vigo" or "where do I get laser eye surgery in Pontevedra" instead of scanning ten Google results. The answer names two or three clinics. A clinic that is not named does not exist for that patient. Clinics have no way to see what the assistants say about them, why, or how to change it, and no way to count the patients that arrive through this channel.

## Who it is for

Private healthcare clinics and small clinic groups (dental, aesthetic, fertility, ophthalmology, physiotherapy) in Galicia, starting in Vigo and Pontevedra, and the marketing agencies that serve them. See `02-icp-and-niche.md`.

## What it does (MVP scope)

1. Probes the main assistants weekly with real patient questions for the clinic's speciality and city.
2. Shows the clinic its usage-weighted share of voice versus named competitors, and the sources the assistants cite.
3. Turns the gap between "sources cited" and "where the clinic is present" into a ranked action list.
4. Attributes patients to the AI channel through three signals (tagged referrals, branded-search uplift, "how did you hear about us").

## What it is not

- Not a generic SEO suite. Not a content farm. Not an ad network.
- Not a promise of guaranteed placement. Nobody can buy a position in an LLM answer today.
- Not a horizontal "AI visibility" dashboard competing with Profound, Peec or Semrush. Those exist and are better funded; see `01-market-critique.md`.

## Why now

- Referral traffic from AI assistants is small but growing fast and converts better than organic search (see `01-market-critique.md` sources).
- 38 % of Spanish residents already use generative AI regularly; ChatGPT reaches ~79 % of Spanish AI users (see `06-models-costs-and-usage-share.md`).
- Local healthcare answers are currently dominated by directories, not clinics — the answer is "unowned" in most specialities (see `03-local-market-vigo-pontevedra.md`).

## Why us

- Founder is based in Galicia with a software background and an existing agency relationship (Tremendev) that already serves local clients.
- The niche is small enough that funded horizontal players will not build for it, and large enough (≈200 dental clinics in Vigo alone) to reach the first 30 customers without leaving the region.

## Success looks like (12 months)

30 paying clinics in Galicia and northern Portugal, ~8 000 € MRR, monthly churn below 4 %, at least one agency reselling, and a proprietary dataset of which sources move LLM answers in healthcare.
