# 08 — Glossary

- **AEO / GEO** — Answer Engine Optimisation / Generative Engine Optimisation: practices to get a brand selected and cited in AI-generated answers. Used interchangeably in the market.
- **AI Overviews / AI Mode** — Google's AI-generated answers inside the search results page. Not counted in chatbot market-share figures; probably the widest-reach AI channel for local queries.
- **Assistant / provider** — a consumer AI product (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity) or Google AI Overviews. "Provider" in code.
- **Attribution** — assigning a visit, call or patient to the AI channel via referral tags, branded-search uplift or a reception answer.
- **Branded-search uplift** — increase in Google searches for the clinic's own name, read from Search Console; the main hidden path from an LLM mention to a visit.
- **Citation / cited source** — a URL the assistant names as the basis of its answer.
- **Concierge MVP** — delivering the service manually to the first customers before building software.
- **Gap** — a cited source where the clinic is absent or incomplete, a patient intent with no matching clinic page, or a technical blocker (robots.txt, schema, NAP).
- **Mention** — the clinic being named in an answer.
- **NAP** — Name, Address, Phone consistency across the web; helps machines resolve mentions to one entity.
- **Position** — order of the clinic among clinics named in a single answer.
- **Probe** — a scheduled batch of prompts sent to providers to sample their answers.
- **Prompt catalogue** — curated set of real patient questions per speciality × city × language.
- **Share of voice (SoV)** — share of answers that mention the clinic; *weighted* SoV multiplies by provider usage share.
- **Usage share / weight** — fraction of assistant usage each provider holds (Similarweb, Comscore, GfK); used to weight SoV.
